Our Story

A local café in a city full of chains

Calton View is a small, independent, women-owned café with a big heart. We pair local and international favourites with the best Scottish ingredients we can find, all hand-picked and sourced from across the country.

Our coffee is a locally and ethically roasted single-origin blend, made with farm-fresh organic Scottish milk. Grab a seat by the window or at one of our outdoor tables when the sun's out, and settle in.

Come as a visitor, leave as a regular ♡
